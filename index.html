Our Story

Your Neighborhood Hangout.

Twins Cafe & Restaurant was built around a simple idea: that a great cafe should feel like a second home. Somewhere you can drop in for a quick coffee, stay for three hours with a laptop, or bring the whole family for dinner on a Sunday.

Every corner is designed to be looked at — and photographed. Every plate is cooked to be remembered. That's the Twins experience.

Good Food Freshly prepared pizzas, pastas, burgers and cafe classics.
Great Coffee A coffee and tea selection that keeps regulars coming back.
Instagrammable Space Interiors made to be photographed — and to feel good in.
Warm Hospitality Cooperative staff, reasonable prices and a welcoming room.
